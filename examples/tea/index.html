Tea — A Field Guide

One plant, six teas, four dials, and one argument nobody wins.

This is an example course. It exists to demonstrate the deck Markdown format and the build-deck.mjs compiler — see the build tooling docs and the source in examples/tea/decks/. It uses the same runtime engine (deck-extras.js) as the real course, so every deck has quizzes, notes, adjustable text, keyboard control, and an accessible mindset — from about 160 lines of Markdown per deck.
T1

What Tea Is

One plant, six teas, and why your water matters more than your leaves.

  • Name the plant all true tea comes from and what separates the six types
  • Describe how oxidation changes a leaf's colour and flavour
  • Explain why water chemistry is the largest single lever in the cup
 T2

Brewing a Proper Cup

Four dials — leaf, water, temperature, time — and the milk debate.

  • Set a sensible ratio and adjust strength without over-steeping
  • Match water temperature to the tea type
  • Diagnose a bitter, weak or stewed cup and name the fix
  • Summarise the milk-in-first argument, etiquette and chemistry
