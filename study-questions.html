"Please read and tick each statement below. All five required items must be checked before you can proceed."
Required consent items * — all five must be checked
  • I understand that I am contributing to an academic research study on AI-assisted coding practitioner patterns.
  • I understand that my submission will be stored anonymously using a randomly generated ID. No name, email, or other identifying information is collected or linked to my submission.
  • I confirm that the transcript I am submitting does not contain personal information (names, email addresses, credentials, or other identifying information about myself or others).
  • I understand that my anonymised data will be stored securely on EU-based servers (Railway, Amsterdam) for up to 10 years after the study closes, in accordance with GDPR Article 89 research exemption, and will then be securely deleted.
  • I understand that I may withdraw my submission at any time before data analysis begins by contacting the researcher using my anonymous ID. Withdrawal will result in permanent deletion of my submission.

On upload, the participant can trigger an ACQI analysis (type explicitness, pure function ratio, decomposability, pattern consistency, structural predictability, McCabe complexity, cognitive complexity, LOC, duplication). A PDF report is generated and available for immediate download. The source code is then scheduled for deletion within 24 hours.
